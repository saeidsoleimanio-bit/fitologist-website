# FITologist Brand Guidelines (Web)

## Direction
Premium · Dark · Athletic · Minimal · Cinematic · Luxury.
Should feel like a high-end personal brand — **not** a gym template, SaaS dashboard or neon fitness site.

## Colour
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Near Black | `#050505` | Page background |
| Dark | `#111111` | Surfaces, cards |
| White | `#F5F5F5` | Primary text |
| FITologist Orange | `#FF6A00` | **Accent only** — lines, CTAs, highlights |
| Silver | `#BFC0C2` | Secondary text, borders, labels |

Orange is an accent, never the dominant colour.

## Typography
- Display: **Barlow Condensed** (600/700), uppercase, tight leading — headlines, numbers, labels.
- Body: **Inter** — short paragraphs, form UI.
- Microcopy: uppercase, wide letter-spacing labels.

## Layout
Strong negative space, cinematic photography, sharp typography, hairline borders (`white/10`), controlled gradients, generous section spacing.

## Logo
- Use supplied files exactly (`09` on dark, `08` on light). Never redraw or recolour.
- Crops of the supplied file (icon mark / wordmark) may be used at small sizes.
- Background watermark use: opacity **0.02–0.04**, never dominant.

## Photography rules
Real photographs of Saeid only. Never: stock models, AI people, identity/face/body alteration, muscle enlargement, artificial smiles.
Allowed: responsive crops, positioning, mild exposure/colour correction, optimisation, cinematic overlays.

## Motion
Premium and controlled: hero entrance, scroll reveals, gentle parallax, staggered cards, expanding orange lines, slow watermark drift, CTA hover/tap feedback, smooth anchor scrolling.
No bouncing, spinning, continuous distracting loops or heavy 3D. `prefers-reduced-motion` gets a calm, static version.

## Voice / CTAs
Concise, confident, no hype. Preferred CTAs: START TRAINING · START YOUR TRANSFORMATION · APPLY NOW · APPLY TO TRAIN · WHATSAPP SAEID · VIEW COACHING.
